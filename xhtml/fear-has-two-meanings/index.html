Fear Has Two Meanings

  • Home

  • Fear Has Two Meanings

Forget Everything And Run, Or Face Everything And Rise

  • Directed by Troy Williams
  • Documentary

We share lived experiences through a lens others have been unable to communicate or unwilling to see.

Restorative Media

Fear moves people in two directions. It makes some run — from a room, from a conversation, from what they did. It moves others straight into the hardest thing they will ever face. This documentary work sits with people in that second motion.

Violence, trauma and incarceration are almost always told from the outside, by people who were never in the room. We make films from the inside of that experience instead — with the people who caused harm, the people who carry it, and the families and communities living with both.

The portrayal of disenfranchised and marginalized people in news, movies, music, podcasts and politics does not accurately represent community in culturally productive ways. So we decided to produce our own movies, podcasts and newscasts.

Accountability on camera

Restorative justice asks a different set of questions than a courtroom does. Not what rule was broken, but who was harmed, what they need, and whose obligation it is to help make it right. Those conversations are slow, uncomfortable and rarely tidy — which is exactly why they are worth filming honestly rather than resolving for an audience.

Filmed with, not about

No one here is a subject handed to an outside crew. The people on screen shape how their story is told, and wherever possible they participate in producing it. We recognize ownership of one’s own life story as intellectual property that should primarily benefit the individual who lived it, his or her family, and the community they live in.

That commitment runs through all of our film work. Troy Williams was a cinematographer and participant in the award-winning documentary The Prison Within, which examined restorative justice, violence, trauma, accountability, healing and incarceration — the same ground this project stands on.
