Why Lived Experience Belongs To The People Who Lived It

  • Posted by Troy Williams
  • 05 Nov 2022
  • 18
  • 9

We recognize ownership of one’s own life story as intellectual property that should primarily benefit the individual who lived it, his or her family, and the community they live in.

Faith Elizabeth Fuller

The Formerly Incarcerated Speaker Series is produced by the National Park Service in partnership with Restorative Media, with support from the Golden Gate National Parks Conservancy. It launched on Alcatraz Island on November 5th, 2022.

We recognize ownership of one’s own life story as intellectual property that should primarily benefit the individual who lived it, his or her family, and the community they live in.

The portrayal of disenfranchised and marginalized people in news, movies, music, podcasts and politics does not accurately represent community in culturally productive ways. So we decided to produce our own movies, podcasts and newscasts.
