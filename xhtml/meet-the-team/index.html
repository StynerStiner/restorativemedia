OUR MISSION

Restorative Media is led by people who have been on the receiving end of the stories this country tells about prison — and who decided to produce their own instead.

That is not a biographical detail. It is the qualification. Our work asks people to speak about harm, accountability and healing at the hardest moments of their lives, and that only works when the person holding the camera or the microphone has been there too.

How this team works

The portrayal of disenfranchised and marginalized people in news, movies, music, podcasts and politics does not accurately represent community in culturally productive ways. So we decided to produce our own movies, podcasts and newscasts.

Between them, the people above have run a newsroom inside a prison, come home after decades away, and built the financial and organisational scaffolding that keeps a non-profit standing. That combination is deliberate. Lived experience sets the direction; professional discipline makes the work last.

We share lived experiences through a lens others have been unable to communicate or unwilling to see.

Work with us

We recognize ownership of one’s own life story as intellectual property that should primarily benefit the individual who lived it, his or her family, and the community they live in. If you are a directly impacted person who wants to tell your own story, a partner organisation, or a venue looking to host the Formerly Incarcerated Speaker Series, we want to hear from you.
