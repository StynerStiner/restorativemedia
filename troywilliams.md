## Core organizational identity

Restorative Media is a **nonprofit, healing-centered social impact media organization founded by Troy Williams**.

Its work sits at the intersection of:

- restorative justice
- journalism
- filmmaking
- multimedia production
- narrative justice
- intellectual ownership
- community storytelling
- education
- public dialogue
- formerly incarcerated leadership
- systems-impacted communities
- trauma, healing, accountability, and transformation

**Central philosophy:** people who have actually lived an experience should have the opportunity
to tell that story themselves, own that narrative, participate in producing it, and benefit from
the intellectual property created from their lives.

Restorative Media challenges harmful or incomplete portrayals of marginalized and systems-impacted
communities by producing its own films, interviews, podcasts, journalism, short-form media,
community projects, speaker events, educational conversations, and other narrative work.

## Mission

> Give voice to the wisdom of lived experience, advance intellectual ownership, and script,
> produce, and distribute narratives that inspire transformation.

This wording stays central in meaning. Rewrite surrounding copy where necessary so the concept is
understandable to an ordinary visitor.

### Explain "intellectual ownership" in plain language

A person's lived experience should not simply become raw material for outside media companies,
journalists, institutions or filmmakers. People should have agency over their stories. Whenever
possible, the people who lived those experiences — their families and their communities — should
participate in and benefit from the narratives and intellectual property created from them.

This principle is what distinguishes Restorative Media from traditional **extractive** storytelling.

## Primary areas of work

The site should make these immediately understandable.

### Storytelling & Media
Film, documentary, journalism, video, audio, podcasts, interviews, short films and multimedia
storytelling centered on lived experience.

### Restorative Justice
Stories and programs exploring accountability, trauma, healing, repair, transformation,
reconciliation and community.

### Narrative Ownership
Helping directly impacted people move from being the *subjects* of other people's stories to
becoming creators, storytellers, producers and owners of their narratives.

### Formerly Incarcerated Speaker Series
Creates opportunities for directly impacted people to share insight, history, expertise and lived
experience with the public. Originally launched through a partnership involving **Alcatraz Island /
the National Park Service** (with support from the Golden Gate National Parks Conservancy;
launched November 5, 2022).
